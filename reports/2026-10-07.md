# Benchmark 2026-10-07

Patch set: `0001-a55-k1-v2`, `0002-a55-f8-v3`, `0003-a55-m4-v2` on rdna-boosts `v16-a55e952b8-r8`.  Written with Claude (AI).

## Setup

- GPU: Radeon AI PRO R9700 (gfx1201), Linux, ROCm 10 (TheRock).
- Models: Qwen3.8-27B official unsloth `UD-Q5_K_M`; Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4 (FreedomAISVR).  Draft: Qwen3.8-27B DFlash2 Q4_K_M.
- Server (every row): `-c 40960 -ub 2048 -b 2048 --parallel 1 -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0`, `--spec-type draft-dflash --spec-draft-n-max 5`.
- **all off**: plain rdna-boosts `v16-a55e952b8-r8`, no variable set.
- **all on**: the release plus this repo's three patches (and two bit-identical patches since sent to rdna-boosts), with
  `GGML_LF_KQ_BLAS=fp8 GGML_LF_FA_FP8=1 GGML_LF_MXFP4_DIRECT=blaslt GGML_LF_DFLASH_DEV=1 GGML_LF_DRAFT_UB=512`.
- Prompt: 32,709 tokens.  PG-19 test book 4047, *The Leavenworth Case* (A. K. Green, 1878), chapters I-X, cut at a paragraph end;
  before it "The following is the beginning of a novel.", after it "Write a review of about 300 words: introduce the main characters
  and the plot so far, and explain what makes the novel compelling."; the model's built-in chat template (one user turn; both models'
  templates render it identically, with the built-in thinking instruction).
- Speed: one full-length warm-up, then 3 rounds of [prefill with `n_predict 1`, no prompt cache] + [the same prompt from the cache,
  512 greedy tokens, `ignore_eos`].  Medians; the 3 rounds gave identical tokens in every configuration.
- KLD: reference = official UD-Q5_K_M on plain r8 (all off); its greedy 400-token answer is fixed.  Each configuration prefills the
  prompt, then the answer is fed one token at a time (decode path); KLD(reference || configuration) over the 400 positions.
  Floor = the same model with a one-bit change in K, i.e. "indistinguishable".

## Results

| configuration | prefill t/s | decode t/s | ms / step | draft accepted | KLD mean | KLD p99 | same top-1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q5 all off | 983 | 47.38 | 58.61 | 328 / 904 | (reference) | — | — |
| MXFP4 all off | 1151 | 54.72 | 48.64 | 320 / 946 | 0.0999 | 0.807 | 90.50% |
| **Q5 all on** | **1972 (+101%)** | 47.45 (+0.1%) | 57.59 (−1.7%) | 325 / 925 | **0.0047** | 0.039 | 96.75% |
| **MXFP4 all on** | **2402 (+109%)** | **62.95 (+15%)** | 47.75 (−1.8%) | 342 / 836 | **0.0977** | 0.870 | 90.50% |
| floor (one-bit K) | — | — | — | — | 0.0016 | 0.017 | 97.75% |

- The gain is almost all prefill (about 2x).  One decode step is about 2% faster; the rest of the decode t/s difference comes from
  the draft acceptance of a different output (MXFP4 all on happens to accept more).
- Q5 all on: KLD about 3x the floor (fp8 GEMM), top-1 agreement 96.75%.  MXFP4: the error is the MXFP4 format's own (0.0999 off,
  0.0977 on); the speed-ups add nothing measurable.
- Run-to-run CV over the 3 rounds: prefill 0.05-1.3%, decode 0.9-1.4%.
- Known: MXFP4 all on, the first round's prefill is slower (2349 vs 2402 t/s); under investigation.

# 2026-10-07 基準

本報告由 Claude（AI）撰寫。patch：`0001-a55-k1-v2`、`0002-a55-f8-v3`、`0003-a55-m4-v2`，套在 rdna-boosts `v16-a55e952b8-r8`。

- 硬體：Radeon AI PRO R9700（gfx1201）、Linux、ROCm 10（TheRock）。模型：官方 unsloth Qwen3.8-27B `UD-Q5_K_M`、Qwen3.8-27B MXFP4（FreedomAISVR）；草稿 DFlash2 Q4_K_M。
- 每一列參數相同：`-c 40960 -ub 2048 -b 2048 --parallel 1 -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0`，DFlash 草稿深度 5。
- **全關**：原版 rdna-boosts r8，不設任何變數。**全開**：原版＋本 repo 三個 patch（另含兩個已送 rdna-boosts 的逐位元相同 patch），設 `GGML_LF_KQ_BLAS=fp8 GGML_LF_FA_FP8=1 GGML_LF_MXFP4_DIRECT=blaslt GGML_LF_DFLASH_DEV=1 GGML_LF_DRAFT_UB=512`。
- Prompt：32,709 token，PG-19 測試集第 4047 號《The Leavenworth Case》第 I～X 章，加上英文的書評要求，套模型內建 chat template。
- 速度：完整長度暖機一次，之後 3 輪（prefill、再用快取 greedy 生成 512 token），取中位數；每個設定 3 輪輸出逐 token 相同。
- KLD：參考＝官方 Q5 全關，其 greedy 400 token 回答固定；各設定 prefill 後逐 token 灌入該回答，計算 400 個位置的 KLD。下限＝只擾動 K 的 1 個位元。

| 設定 | prefill t/s | decode t/s | 每步 ms | 草稿接受 | KLD 平均 | KLD p99 | top-1 相同 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q5 全關 | 983 | 47.38 | 58.61 | 328 / 904 | （參考） | — | — |
| MXFP4 全關 | 1151 | 54.72 | 48.64 | 320 / 946 | 0.0999 | 0.807 | 90.50% |
| **Q5 全開** | **1972（+101%）** | 47.45（+0.1%） | 57.59（−1.7%） | 325 / 925 | **0.0047** | 0.039 | 96.75% |
| **MXFP4 全開** | **2402（+109%）** | **62.95（+15%）** | 47.75（−1.8%） | 342 / 836 | **0.0977** | 0.870 | 90.50% |
| 下限（1 位元擾動） | — | — | — | — | 0.0016 | 0.017 | 97.75% |

- 加速幾乎都在 prefill（約 2 倍）；每步 decode 約快 2%，decode t/s 其餘的差異來自輸出不同造成的草稿接受率差異。
- Q5 全開的 KLD 約為下限的 3 倍（fp8 矩陣乘），top-1 相同 96.75%。MXFP4 的誤差來自格式本身（全關 0.0999、全開 0.0977），加速沒有再增加。
- 已知：MXFP4 全開的第 1 輪 prefill 較慢（2349 對 2402 t/s），追查中。
